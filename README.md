Cosuno is an AI‑based tendering platform for the construction industry in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. It connects clients and subcontractors, offering over 4,000 private/commercial tenders per month from 600+ clients, helping contractors win jobs with AI‑assisted bid creation and delivering average cost savings of 5‑7%.

**Areas:** Construction · Marketplace · Artificial Intelligence · Tendering · Germany

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Cosuno: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
